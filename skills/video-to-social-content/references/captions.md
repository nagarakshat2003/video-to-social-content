# Caption Guidelines

Read this when filling in `captions.md` after `captions.py scaffold`. Replace every `TODO`; leave the pre-filled facts (files, timestamps, resolutions, source, rights) as they are.

## Reels

- **Hook:** one punchy opening line that stops the scroll. It must be true to the clip.
- **Caption:** 1–3 sentences of context or story in the tone of the video.
- **CTA:** a call to action, e.g. "Which moment was your favourite? Comment below 👇".
- **Hashtags:** 3–5 relevant tags (`captions.py check` enforces the count).
- **On-screen text:** a short overlay idea, e.g. *"When you realize you forgot to commit…"*.
- **Content flags:** `none`, or a tag such as `[Mature / Violence]` plus the platform risk (age restriction or demonetization on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube) and a cleaner alternative cut point.

## Photo carousels

- **Caption:** 80–200 words telling the story of the moment, mood or insight.
- Keep the slide order from the scaffold unless there is a reason to change it; if you reorder, keep every file listed.
- End with a question or reflection, then hashtags.

## Factual integrity

- Quote only words that appear in the transcript excerpt for that clip (or that are clearly audible). If the transcript is missing or unclear, paraphrase or describe instead of quoting.
- Never invent names, titles, places, numbers or statistics. If a speaker is unknown, say "the speaker" or describe them neutrally.
- No speculative or defamatory claims about identifiable real people beyond what the footage shows.

## Platform notes

- TikTok and Reels: hook in the first line; keep the caption body short.
- YouTube Shorts: the first line doubles as the title, so keep it under ~60 characters.
- Instagram carousels: the first slide carries the hook; the caption can run longer.
